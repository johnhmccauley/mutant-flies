Mutant Fly

A fortune lies in the cellars under the ruins, and something is guarding every level down. You cannot kill it. You can only wall it in — four bricks, one on each side. The cellar wall is no help to you.

    Z X : / or the arrow keys. Bricks shove in whole lines; one driven into a monster is eaten; one pushed off the edge is gone for good.
