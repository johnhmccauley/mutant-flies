The Micro User · July 1985 · John McCauley

Mutant Fly

A fortune lies in the cellars under the ruins, and on every level down there is a fly the size of a man. You cannot kill it. You can only wall it in.

  • Walk into a brick to shove it. A whole line shifts at once.
  • Drive a brick into the fly and its jaws take it. Push one off the edge of the cellar and it is gone for good.
  • Surround it with four bricks to trap it. The cellar wall is no help to you.
  • Fewer bricks each cellar down, and the fly never lets you get far. Touch it once and that is that.
